[5] Begovic et al. (2023): Begovic et al. (2023) [5] presented a survey of cryptographic
ransomware detection techniques and examined approaches based on system calls, input/output
activity, and file-system monitoring. The authors identified file-system activity as an important
indicator for detecting ransomware behavior. This research supports the present File Integrity
Tester because monitoring changes in protected files can provide an early indication of suspicious
activity.
[6] Satilmis et al. (2024): Satilmis et al. (2024) [6] conducted a systematic literature review of
Host-Based Intrusion Detection Systems and analyzed research published between 2020 and 2023.
The study examined different HIDS approaches, their advantages, limitations, and future research
directions. The findings highlight the continuing importance of host-level monitoring for
cybersecurity. This is relevant to the present project because the proposed system provides a simple
file-level monitoring mechanism within a host environment.
[7] Chew et al. (2024): Chew et al. (2024) [7] investigated ransomware detection using system-
call behavior and real-time monitoring. Their research focused on identifying malicious activities
before extensive damage could occur. Although the methodology differs from the hash-based
approach of the present project, it demonstrates the importance of timely monitoring of file-related
activities. Real-time monitoring can therefore be considered a possible future enhancement to the
proposed File Integrity Tester.
[8] Bönninghausen et al. (2024): Bönninghausen et al. (2024) [8] presented a comprehensive
survey of intrusion-detection datasets and discussed challenges associated with outdated datasets
and security-system evaluation. The study emphasized the importance of realistic and reliable
evaluation methods for cybersecurity solutions. This is relevant to the present project because
systematic testing with modified, added, deleted, and unchanged files is necessary to evaluate the
effectiveness of the File Integrity Tester.
